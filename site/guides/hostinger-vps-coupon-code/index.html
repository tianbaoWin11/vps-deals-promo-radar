What the official VPS tab actually showed

We selected VPS hosting, not the default Web hosting tab, on Hostinger's US English coupons page. Each of its KVM 1, 2, 4 and 8 cards showed COUPONSPAGE as applied. The code is a dated observation on the provider's own page; we did not complete a checkout, so we cannot promise it will be accepted for a later order or another region.

Hostinger's separate VPS product page showed different plan-card prices when checked in the same session. That difference is a reason to compare the exact route and cart you will use, not a reason to add the displayed discounts together. The two pages show monthly equivalents; the VPS page explicitly says plans are paid upfront.

Choose the server before the discount

The provider's VPS product page listed KVM 1 with 1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe storage and 4 TB bandwidth. KVM 2 listed 2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB NVMe storage and 8 TB bandwidth. KVM 4 and KVM 8 appeared as larger tiers. These are advertised plan resources, not performance measurements from our own server tests.

Our decision check is to write down the application's memory, storage and transfer needs first, select the smallest advertised tier that meets them, and only then compare that tier's coupon and ordinary product-page routes. A larger percentage off an unsuitable tier does not make it a better purchase. If the application needs a particular operating system or data-center region, verify those options in the chosen plan's cart as well.

Work out what you will really owe

The coupon card separately displays an introductory monthly equivalent and a renewal amount; the product page says the plan is paid upfront. Before payment, record four different fields from the actual cart: selected KVM tier, term, amount due now and renewal amount. Do not treat the small per-month label as the amount charged today.

If the official cart removes the code or changes the term, use the cart's current total rather than this article's October 11 snapshot. We did not enter payment information or verify a completed order. Prices and taxes can depend on the buyer's location, and Hostinger's page states its listed prices exclude VAT.

Does the VPS offer include a domain?

The VPS product page included a free-domain feature. Its expanded condition specified a .cloud or .tech domain for the first year with a VPS plan lasting at least 12 months. That is narrower than a blanket promise of a free domain and does not establish a free .com name.

If a domain matters to the purchase, check its exact extension, registration period and later renewal in the VPS cart. Keep that separate from a standalone domain purchase. The coupon label on a VPS card is not proof that it discounts a separately registered domain.
