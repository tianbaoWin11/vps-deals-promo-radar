Privacy Policy

How VPS Deals handles information when you read this site or email its editor.

Last reviewed October 9, 2026.

Information you provide

This site has no account registration or contact form. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use it to respond to your message.

Hosting and page resources

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this site and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, for delivery and security. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may connect to Google's servers when you open a page.

Optional site analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and page views only after you choose “Allow analytics.” If you decline, this site does not load the Google Analytics tag. The tag may use cookies and send usage information to Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not send your email correspondence to Analytics. We save your allow or decline choice in your browser's local storage; use “Cookie choices” in the footer to change it. If you withdraw permission, the site reloads without the Analytics tag for future visits.

Ads and affiliate links

We plan to display third-party ads, but no advertising code has been added yet. If ads are enabled, the advertising provider may collect information or use cookies under its own policies; we will update this page with the relevant provider and choices before enabling that code. Some links may become affiliate links after approval. We will identify those links when active and may earn a commission if you make a purchase through them.

Links to other sites

Provider and source links lead to external websites. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit them.
