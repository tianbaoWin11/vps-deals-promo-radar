ABOUT THIS SITE

About VPS Deals

VPS Deals is maintained by a site editor who tracks public VPS and cloud hosting offers. This is an independent editorial site, not a hosting provider.

What we publish

Our watch list points to official provider pages. An offer appears as current only when our source check confirms the claim. Each offer page shows the source, the check time, and the terms we could verify. If a check fails or the stated offer expires, we stop presenting that offer as current.

How to use the information

Promotional credit is not the same as a plan price. Before signing up, open the provider's own page and confirm eligibility, billing, and current terms. The comparison page counts offers we have verified; it does not rank providers by quality or price.

We plan to display third-party ads. Some links may later be affiliate links, and those links will be identified when active. Advertising or a potential commission does not replace our requirement to show an official source for an offer.

Have a question or found an outdated claim? Contact the site editor.
